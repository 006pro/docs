# Loan Management System – Business Rules

1. Every customer must have a unique Customer ID.
2. Customers must be at least 18 years old.
3. KYC verification is mandatory before loan approval.
4. A customer cannot submit duplicate pending loan applications.
5. Loan amount and tenure must be within predefined limits.
6. Credit score and monthly income must be verified.
7. Loan eligibility must be calculated automatically.
8. Only authorized users can approve or reject loans.
9. Every rejection must include a reason.
10. Approved loans must have a unique Loan Account Number.
11. EMI must be calculated automatically based on interest rate and tenure.
12. Every loan must have a repayment schedule.
13. Late payments must incur penalties according to the loan agreement.
14. Every payment must have a unique transaction ID.
15. Only approved loans can be disbursed.
16. Loan disbursement must be recorded.
17. Loans can be closed only after all outstanding dues are cleared.
18. All financial transactions must be recorded in audit logs.
19. Users must have role-based access permissions.
20. Customer financial information must be protected and encrypted.
